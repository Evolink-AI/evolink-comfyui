# EvoLink Seedance 2.5 (Official)

ByteDance Seedance 2.5 / 2.0 (standard / fast / mini) text-to-video & image-to-video. Synced audio at no extra cost.

- **Model choice sets the mode**: *-text-to-video / *-image-to-video (first_frame, optionally last_frame)
- **duration**: 2.5 supports 4–30 s or -1 (auto); 2.0 family 4–15 s
- **quality**: 480p budget tier up to 1080p; 4k on 2.0 standard only; fast/mini top out at 720p
- For reference-to-video / video-edit / video-extend use the "EvoLink Video (All Models)" node with extra_params
