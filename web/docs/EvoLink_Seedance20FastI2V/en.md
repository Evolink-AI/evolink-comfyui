# EvoLink Seedance 2.0 (Official)

ByteDance Seedance 2.0 / 2.0 Fast text-to-video & image-to-video. Synced audio at no extra cost.

- **Model choice sets the mode**: *-text-to-video / *-image-to-video (first_frame, optionally last_frame)
- **duration**: any integer 4-15 s
- **quality**: 480p budget tier up to 4K (1080p/4k on standard only; fast tops out at 720p with automatic fallback)
- For longer clips (up to 30 s / auto duration) use the Seedance 2.5 node; for the lowest cost use Seedance 2.0 Mini
