# EvoLink Sora 2 (Official)

OpenAI Sora 2 / Sora 2 Pro text-to-video & image-to-video. VIDEO output connects straight to the native Save Video node.

- **duration**: 4 / 8 / 12 s, billed by length; **aspect_ratio**: 16:9 or 9:16
- **quality**: Pro only, 720p/1080p (1080p ≈ 1.67x price)
- **image-to-video**: connect the image input; for sora-2-preview the reference is auto-resized to exactly match the aspect ratio. No photos of real people
- Sora moderation is strict; failed tasks are not billed
